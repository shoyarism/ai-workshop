## Setup Steps

### Step 1: Choose a Niche
**High-engagement faceless niches**:
- **Motivational quotes** (simple, high share rate)
- **Reddit story summaries** (r/AskReddit, r/relationship_advice, r/interestingasfuck)
- **AI tips & tools** (tech-savvy audience, high affiliate potential)
- **Business/finance facts** (high CPM for ads)
- **Productivity hacks** (broad appeal)
- **Crypto/tech news** (enthusiastic community)

### Step 2: Set Up Automation
```python
# Daily content pipeline
1. TREND DISCOVERY:
   - Scrape r/all trending posts
   - Monitor Twitter trending topics
   - Check what's viral on TikTok

2. CONTENT GENERATION:
   - LLM creates 5–10 tweet/thread variations
   - Humanizer removes robotic patterns
   - Canva generates quote images (if applicable)

3. SCHEDULING:
   - Use Hypefury/Typefully to schedule posts
   - Post at optimal times (7–9 AM, 12–2 PM, 6–9 PM local time)

4. ENGAGEMENT:
   - Auto-reply to top comments (AI-generated but personalized)
   - Auto-retweet similar accounts in niche
```

### Step 3: Monetization Setup
1. **Affiliate links**: Amazon Associates, software tools (Notion, ClickUp, etc.)
2. **Sponsored posts**: Reach out to brands once you have 10k+ followers
3. **Digital products**: Sell templates, presets, or courses
4. **Account flipping**: Build and sell accounts on Flippa ($5k–$50k)

### Step 4: Scale
- Target: 5–10 posts per day
- Goal: 10k followers in 3 months, 100k in 12 months
- Cross-post to Twitter, Instagram, TikTok for maximum reach

## Workflow & Best Practices

### Daily Workflow
| Time | Task | Automation Level |
|------|------|-----------------|
| Morning (15 min) | Review trending topics, approve top 10 content ideas | Manual |
| Midday (automated) | Posts go live via scheduler | 100% automated |
| Afternoon (15 min) | Engage with top comments, reply to DMs | Manual |
| Evening (automated) | Analyze performance, adjust tomorrow's schedule | 100% automated |

### Content Quality Standards
- **Tweets**: Under 280 characters, hook in first 10 words
- **Threads**: 5–10 tweets, clear structure (hook → body → CTA)
- **Images**: High contrast, bold text, on-brand color scheme
- **Videos**: 15–60 seconds, auto-generated with captions

### Growth Hacks
- **Engagement pods**: Join pods of similar-sized accounts to boost engagement
- **Trendjacking**: Post within 1 hour of major news/trends
- **Controversy**: Take polarizing stances to spark debate
- **Giveaways**: Run "follow + retweet to win" campaigns

### Warning Signs
- **Shadowban**: Avoid spammy behavior, don't use banned hashtags
- **Account suspension**: Follow platform TOS, don't buy followers
- **Low engagement**: Refresh content strategy, test new formats
- **Copyright strikes**: Use royalty-free music and visuals
