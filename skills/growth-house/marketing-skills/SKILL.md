## 2. Copywriting Invariants

1. **Specific Beats Vague:** Never say *"Our tool helps you work faster"*. Say *"Cuts continuous integration build times from 14 minutes to 82 seconds"*.
2. **Benefit Over Feature:** A feature is *"AES-256 local encryption"*; the benefit is *"Your proprietary source code never leaves your laptop"*.
3. **The "So What?" Test:** Read every claim and ask "So what?". If the sentence does not immediately answer what the customer gains, delete or rewrite it.
4. **Zero AI Marketing Tropes:** Banish hyperbole, inflated claims, and generic AI platitudes. Focus on tangible proofs and deterministic capabilities.
