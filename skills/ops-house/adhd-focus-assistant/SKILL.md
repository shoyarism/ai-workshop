# ADHD Executive Function & Focus Engine

Provides structured strategies and workflows designed to reduce executive friction, prevent cognitive overwhelm, and maintain sustained momentum during complex technical tasks.

## Core Methodologies
1. **Micro-Chunking**: Break complex architectural tasks into <= 5-minute atomic steps.
2. **Deterministic Checkpoints**: Commit and verify after every micro-step to remove mental overhead.
3. **Low-Cognitive-Load Documentation**: High density, bulleted, clear action items with zero fluff.
