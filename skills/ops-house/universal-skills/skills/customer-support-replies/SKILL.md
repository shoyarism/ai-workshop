# Customer Support Replies

1. Acknowledge the issue in one line. Do not argue.
2. Give the answer or the next step first.
3. Be accurate. If you do not know, say you will check and when you will reply.
4. Keep a calm, kind tone. No blame, no jargon.
5. For complaints: apologise for the experience, state what you will do, give a contact.
6. Never share private information. Do not give medical, legal or financial conclusions beyond official guidance.
