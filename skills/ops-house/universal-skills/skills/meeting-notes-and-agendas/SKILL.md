# Meeting Notes And Agendas

Agenda: goal, topics with time, who leads each, decisions needed.
Notes: date, attendees, decisions, action items (owner and due date), open questions.
- Record decisions and actions first; discussion after.
- Be neutral and accurate. Do not invent attributions.
- Send a follow-up with the action list within a day.
