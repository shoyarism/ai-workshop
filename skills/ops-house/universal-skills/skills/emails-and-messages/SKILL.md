# Emails And Messages

- Subject: specific and short. Opening: why you are writing, in one line.
- Body: what you need, key facts, any deadline. Short paragraphs.
- Close with one clear ask and the next step.
- Match formality to the relationship. When unsure, polite and plain.
- For hard messages (decline, complaint, bad news): be direct, kind, no blame, offer an alternative.
- Do not invent details. Leave [brackets] for unknown names and dates.
- Offer one short version unless the user wants options.
