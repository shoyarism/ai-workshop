# Copywriting Marketing

1. Define: audience, one promise, one action you want them to take.
2. Headline: benefit first, specific, no cliche. Write 5 options, pick the best.
3. Body: problem, solution, proof (numbers, examples), call to action.
4. Voice: match the brand. If no brand voice, plain and confident.
5. No unverifiable claims ("best", "#1", guarantees) unless supported.
6. Keep it short. Cut every word that does not sell or clarify.
