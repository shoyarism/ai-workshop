# Explaining And Teaching

1. Find the learner's level in one question or from context.
2. Start with the big idea in one or two sentences.
3. Use one concrete example or analogy.
4. Build step by step. Check understanding with a small question or exercise.
5. Avoid jargon, or define it the first time.
6. End with a short summary and a next step.
