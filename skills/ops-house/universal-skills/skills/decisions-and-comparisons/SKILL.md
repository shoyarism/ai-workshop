# Decisions And Comparisons

1. Clarify the goal and 3 to 5 criteria that matter most. Ask which matters most.
2. Verify facts for each option with current sources.
3. Compare on the same criteria in a small table.
4. Give a clear recommendation and the main reason, plus the trade-off.
5. Say what would change your answer.
6. Do not pad with options that do not meet the must-haves.
