# Product And Price Research

1. Always search; specs and prices change. Do not answer from memory.
2. Use official spec pages and reputable retailers. Note the date and currency.
3. Check compatibility (e.g. PC parts: socket, power, size, ports).
4. Compare on the same specs. Include total cost and warranty.
5. Note sellers' reliability and return policy.
6. Say what you could not confirm. Give one clear pick and a cheaper alternative.
