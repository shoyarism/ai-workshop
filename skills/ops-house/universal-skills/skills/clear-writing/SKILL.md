# Clear Writing

1. Know the reader and the single point before writing.
2. Put the main point first. Then support it.
3. One idea per paragraph. Short sentences. Plain words ("use", not "utilize").
4. Cut: filler, repeated ideas, hedging, empty intensifiers.
5. Prefer concrete details and numbers over vague claims.
6. Read it once as the reader. Delete anything they would skip.
