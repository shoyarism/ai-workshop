# Pr And Comms

1. Facts first: who, what, when, where, why it matters.
2. Press release: headline, lead paragraph with the news, supporting detail, a quote (only if given or approved), boilerplate, contact.
3. Tone: factual, calm, no hype. Avoid superlatives you cannot prove.
4. Never invent quotes, statistics or names. Mark gaps as [to confirm].
5. Sensitive topics (health, incidents, complaints): be accurate, empathetic, avoid speculation, and recommend approval by the responsible person before publishing.
6. Adapt the same news to channels: press, social, internal.
