# Summarizing

1. State the purpose of the summary (decision, briefing, notes).
2. Lead with the conclusion or main point.
3. Keep key facts, numbers, decisions, owners and dates. Drop detail and repetition.
4. Keep the source's order of importance, not its structure.
5. Never add information that is not in the source. Flag anything unclear.
6. Length: about 10 to 20 percent of the original, unless told otherwise.
