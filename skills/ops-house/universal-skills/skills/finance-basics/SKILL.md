# Finance Basics

- Give general information, not personalised financial advice. Say you are not a financial advisor when advice is implied.
- Show formulas and assumptions: interest rate, term, fees, tax.
- Verify current rates, fees and rules with an official source. They change.
- Compare total cost, not just monthly payment.
- For freelance pricing: hours x rate plus overhead, a margin and a revision limit.
- Flag risks (high interest, hidden fees, unrealistic returns).
