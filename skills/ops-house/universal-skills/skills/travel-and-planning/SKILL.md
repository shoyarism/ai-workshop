# Travel And Planning

- Ask or assume: dates, budget, number of people, interests, pace.
- Verify current opening hours, prices, visas and transport. They change.
- Group by area to reduce travel time. Leave slack.
- Give a day-by-day outline with times, and a short backup plan.
- Flag booking deadlines and documents needed.
