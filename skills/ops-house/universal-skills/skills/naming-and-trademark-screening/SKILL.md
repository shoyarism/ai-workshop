# Naming And Trademark Screening

Naming:
1. Prefer distinctive coined or uncommon words over common words. Short, easy to spell and say.
2. Avoid names that sound like big existing brands or common categories.
Screening each candidate:
1. Search the exact name in quotes, and with the industry word (e.g. "Name AI", "Name software").
2. Search sound-alikes and near spellings.
3. Check for companies, products, apps, packages and social handles.
4. Trademarks: classes 9, 35 and 42 for software; look at official databases (USPTO, WIPO, and the user's national office such as DJKI for Indonesia). Web search cannot see pending filings.
5. Domain: check .com and .ai in a registrar. Do not claim a domain is free unless you checked a registrar.
Report: clear, risky, or rejected, with one-line reasons. Never say a name is guaranteed free.
