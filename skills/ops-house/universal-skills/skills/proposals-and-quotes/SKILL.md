# Proposals And Quotes

Include: the client's goal in their words, scope (what is and is not included), deliverables, timeline, price and payment terms, number of revisions, what you need from the client, next step.
- Be specific about scope to avoid creep.
- Price by value and effort, not guesswork. Show options if useful (basic, standard, premium).
- Keep it to one or two pages. Plain, confident tone.
- Never promise results you cannot control.
