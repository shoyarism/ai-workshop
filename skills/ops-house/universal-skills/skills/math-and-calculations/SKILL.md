# Math And Calculations

- Write the formula and the numbers, then compute with a tool or script when more than trivial.
- Keep units and carry them through. Convert at the start.
- Sanity check: is the answer the right size? Estimate roughly first.
- Round only at the end. State the rounding.
- For money, state the currency and whether it includes tax or fees.
- Show the steps briefly so the user can verify.
