# Social Graphics And Posters

- Sizes: Instagram feed 1080x1350 (portrait) or 1080x1080; stories 1080x1920; keep important content inside safe margins (about 6 to 8 percent).
- One message. Big headline, short supporting line, one action.
- Brand: logo placed consistently, brand colours, brand fonts.
- Text over images needs contrast (overlay or solid block).
- Print: confirm size, bleed (usually 3 mm) and colour mode. Screens use RGB, print uses CMYK.
- Export at the right size and format and name files clearly.
