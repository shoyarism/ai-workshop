# Hospital Color Coding & Signage Accessibility Skill

## Overview
Provides guidelines for healthcare facility color coding, zone identification, signage system design, and Color Vision Deficiency (CVD / Buta Warna) compliance based on KARS and international hospital standards (ISO 7010 & WCAG 2.1 AAA).

## 11-Zone Standard Palette Matrix for Healthcare Facilities

## Verification Rules
1. **No Duplicates**: No two zones may share identical or visually confusing color families (e.g. Lab vs Lobby must be distinct; Farmasi vs Jenazah must be distinct).
2. **CVD Compatibility**: Every color MUST be paired with a unique geometric symbol to ensure 100% usability for color-blind patients.
3. **Contrast Ratio**: Text on signage background must meet WCAG AAA (contrast ratio >= 7:1).
