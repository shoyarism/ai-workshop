# NovaFlow AI — Design Direction

## Subject
Developer tool that turns natural language into production backend APIs. Users are engineers and technical founders who ship fast.

## Audience
Engineering leads, technical founders, indie hackers. They value speed, clarity, no-nonsense execution.

## Page Kind
Product launch / trailer video (26s, 1080p, no UI to interact with — narrative-driven)

## Visual Language
**Print-meets-terminal**: editorial warmth meets technical precision. Think *The Verge* meets *Linear* — warm paper backgrounds, bold condensed typography, one sharp accent color used sparingly, geometric precision.

## Dials
- **ENERGY 2** (balanced): confident but not aggressive, clean but not sterile
- **RHYTHM 2** (varied): scenes change composition intentionally — no uniform template
- **MOTION 2** (scroll-reveal): deliberate reveals, smooth transitions, purposeful timing

## Palette
- `--cream`: #F5F2EB (warm paper base)
- `--ink`: #1C1917 (near-black, warm charcoal)
- `--accent`: #DC2626 (Indian red, one deliberate accent)
- `--muted`: #78716C (warm gray for secondary text)
- `--paper-dark`: #E7E5E4 (slightly darker paper for contrast)

## Typography
- **Display**: "Playfair Display" — editorial serif for headlines, used sparingly (1-2 per scene)
- **Body**: "Inter" — clean sans for body, labels, CTAs
- **Mono**: "JetBrains Mono" — small calls only (timestamps, code hints)

## Identity Motif
**The red line**: a thin horizontal rule (#DC2626) that appears in every scene transition and as an underline accent. It's the visual thread tying the video together.

## Motion Rules
- No endless loops
- Each animation serves comprehension (reveal information progressively)
- Transitions: black wipe from bottom (scene break), white fade (scene intro)
- Easing: GSAP power2.out for entrances, power3.inOut for transitions
- Duration: 0.4-0.8s for most elements, never longer than needed

## Copy
- Specific, concrete, no buzzwords
- No "AI-powered", "seamless", "revolutionary"
- Active voice, short sentences
- One deliberate line per scene
